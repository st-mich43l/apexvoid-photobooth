# ApexVoid Photobooth 0.3.0

A feature release on top of 0.2.1: a redesigned Gallery session screen.

## What changed

- **Gallery session detail is now a photography workspace.** A compact header shows the session's date and time, status,
  photo count, frame and print format, with **Export session** as the main action and an overflow menu (copy ID, move to
  trash; trash still asks first and can be undone).
- **Original photos and the final image side by side.** The originals are shown uncropped in an adaptive grid; a dedicated
  panel shows the complete finished frame with Print, Export and Change design, and a selector when a session has several
  compositions. Video-only sessions show their recordings instead of an empty frame panel. Narrow and portrait screens stack
  with the finished image first.
- **Print history at a glance:** job count, printer, paper, copies, status, reprint, and clear loading, error and empty
  states, with the note that Windows reports a job as sent, not that paper came out.
- Export options (including edited photos) moved into the export dialog. English and Vietnamese texts for the new screen.
- Release publishing now pins the full publisher revision.

## Known limitations

- **Hardware validation:** camera, printer and touchscreen behaviour has not been separately accepted on physical hardware for
  this release. The Canon live view and the final photo may show different areas on some bodies; check once on your camera
  (`docs/BOOTH_CAPTURE_GEOMETRY.md`).
- Not code-signed; Windows SmartScreen may warn on first run. Verify the SHA-256 in `SHA256SUMS`.
- Everything else in the 0.2.0 and 0.2.1 notes still applies.
