# ApexVoid Photobooth 0.2.1

A hotfix for photo framing and photo selection in Booth Mode, found by testing 0.2.0 with a Canon EOS R100.

## What changed

- **The camera framing guide no longer changes or disappears.** With the default of eight candidate photos, the guide used
  to vanish for the later shots on frames with fewer photos (for example shots 7 and 8 on a six-photo sheet, or shots 4 to 8
  on a three-photo strip). It now comes from the frame's real photo windows, is fixed before the first shot and is the same
  for every shot. Frames with differently shaped windows use the area all of them show.
- **Choosing photos is now one tap.** After the shots, every photo is shown (eight in a 4×2 grid on landscape screens). Tap
  a photo to select it and again to unselect it; numbers show the order in the frame. Continue becomes available when
  exactly the number of photos the frame needs is selected. The frame-slot buttons, automatic pre-selection and the
  per-photo **Retake** buttons are gone from Booth Mode (Studio keeps retake for the operator).
- **Safer saving.** Photos that were not chosen are deleted only after the finished image has been saved; if the image
  cannot be made, every photo is kept. A second tap on Continue can no longer start a second composition. Changing the
  frame while choosing now correctly changes how many photos are needed.
- English and Vietnamese texts for the new screen; obsolete retake wording removed.

## Known limitations

- **Canon live view versus final photo:** the guide assumes the live view and the full-resolution JPEG show the same area. Canon
  does not guarantee this for every body and mode. Check it once on your camera (`docs/BOOTH_CAPTURE_GEOMETRY.md`).
- **Hardware validation:** The framing issue was observed while testing with a Canon EOS R100. This hotfix has not been separately accepted on physical hardware.
- Not code-signed; Windows SmartScreen may warn on first run. Verify the SHA-256 in `SHA256SUMS`.
- Everything else in the 0.2.0 notes still applies, except that guests can no longer retake individual photos.
